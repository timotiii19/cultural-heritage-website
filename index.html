PROFILE / 01
A FACE BEHIND THE STORY
PROJECT ROLE / Project Developer

Arabella Valerio

Hi! I'm Arabella, one of the developers of Pamana, a website dedicated to showcasing and preserving the rich culture and heritage of the Philippines. As part of the development team, I contributed to building and improving the website to make it informative, accessible, and engaging for users.

As an IT student, I am passionate about using technology to create meaningful projects that can educate and inspire others. For me, Filipino heritage represents our identity, traditions, and the stories passed down through generations. Through Pamana, I hope to help more people, especially the younger generation, appreciate our cultural roots and recognize the importance of preserving them for the future.

PROFILE / 02
A FACE BEHIND THE STORY
PROJECT ROLE / Project Developer

J.C. Roeven Peji

Hi! I'm Roeven, an Information Technology student who enjoys exploring technology, web development, and creative design. I've always been curious about how things work, especially when it comes to technology and creating something from scratch. I enjoy experimenting with new ideas, learning different skills, and finding ways to express my creativity through my work. Although challenges are part of the process, I see them as opportunities to learn, improve, and become better at what I do. As one of the developers of PAMANA, I had the opportunity to combine my technical skills with my creativity and appreciation for Filipino culture. Working on this project allowed me to explore different approaches to web design while collaborating with my fellow developers. For me, PAMANA is more than just an academic requirement; it's a reflection of our teamwork, creativity, and dedication to creating something meaningful that represents our cultural identity.
