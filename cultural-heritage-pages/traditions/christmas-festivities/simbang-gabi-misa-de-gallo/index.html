01 — THE TRADITION

Simbang Gabi / Misa de Gallo

Nine days of Mass before Christmas.

A nine-day religious tradition of dawn Masses held in preparation for Christmas.

Place of origin

Philippines, influenced by Spanish Catholic traditions.

Date of celebration

December 16–24 (nine consecutive days).

HOW IT STARTED

Simbang Gabi began during the Spanish colonial period when Catholic missionaries introduced dawn Masses so Filipino farmers and workers could attend church before starting their daily activities. It developed into a nine-day religious tradition in preparation for Christmas.

Dawn Masses let farmers and workers attend church before starting their daily activities.

MEANING / MESSAGE CONVEYED

It represents faith, devotion, sacrifice, hope, and spiritual preparation for the birth of Jesus Christ.

ESSENCE / IMPORTANCE / RELEVANCE

Simbang Gabi strengthens Filipino Catholic traditions and brings communities together through prayer and worship. It also reflects the strong religious faith of many Filipinos and their anticipation of Christmas.

HOW THE TRADITION TRAVELS
  1. 01 Spain to the Philippines

    Catholic missionaries introduced dawn Masses during the Spanish colonial period.

  2. 02 Across the islands

    Farmers and workers attended before their daily activities, and the custom grew into nine days of preparation for Christmas.

  3. 03 Around the world

    Filipino communities overseas keep it alive; the Philippine Mission in Geneva has observed Simbang Gabi too.
