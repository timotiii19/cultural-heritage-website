Courtesy carried in conversation 4 references

Po & Opo

In Tagalog, po is a politeness marker commonly used in respectful address, while opo is a respectful way to say yes. Their meaning depends on the speaker, listener, and situation; courtesy also comes through tone, titles, and attentive listening.

GREETING / 02 02 ↗
A greeting that honors elders 7 references

Pagmamano

Pagmamano is a Filipino greeting gesture associated with showing respect to an elder. The younger person receives the elder's offered hand and gently brings its back to the forehead, often saying "mano po."

COMMUNITY / 03 03 ↗
Many hands, a shared purpose 4 references

Bayanihan

Bayanihan describes people joining their effort to help a neighbor or accomplish a shared task. Moving a house is its familiar image; cooperation and mutual aid give the idea meaning in community life today.
