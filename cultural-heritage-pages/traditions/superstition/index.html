FAREWELL / THE JOURNEY HOME

Pagpag

A pause between the wake and home.

Some mourners make a short stop elsewhere before going home after a wake or funeral. The practice is understood by some families as a way to leave the presence of the dead behind; details vary from one family to another.

FAMILY / TIMING & FORTUNE

Sukob

When family milestones share a year.

In commonly described versions of sukob, siblings avoid marrying in the same calendar year, or a wedding is delayed during a close family member's mourning. The belief reflects how some families weigh timing, care, and shared fortune.

HOME / A COUNT OF THREE

Oro, Plata, Mata

A staircase counted in three.

Each step is counted in a repeating sequence: oro (gold), plata (silver), mata (death or misfortune). In the belief, a staircase should end on oro or plata rather than mata. The practice and its explanations differ across families and places.
