PROJECT ROLE / Project Developer

Arabella Valerio

Hi! I'm Arabella, one of the developers of Pamana, a website dedicated to showcasing and preserving the rich culture and heritage of the Philippines. As part of the development team, I contributed to building and improving the website to make it informative, accessible, and engaging for users.

As an IT student, I am passionate about using technology to create meaningful projects that can educate and inspire others. For me, Filipino heritage represents our identity, traditions, and the stories passed down through generations. Through Pamana, I hope to help more people, especially the younger generation, appreciate our cultural roots and recognize the importance of preserving them for the future.

PROFILE / 04
A FACE BEHIND THE STORY
PROJECT ROLE / Project Developer

Juan Miguel Campomanes

Hi! I'm Juan Miguel, an Information Technology student and one of the developers of Pamana. I built and designed the Christmas Festivities pages, covering Simbang Gabi, Noche Buena, and the Filipino Christmas delicacies, and I also worked on the Festivals page. For each story I researched the traditions, wrote the content, gathered the references and photo credits, and added the interactive scenes, illustrations, and festive details that make the season feel alive.

I enjoy turning cultural stories into experiences people can explore, not just read. To me, Filipino heritage is the way we gather, celebrate, and share food and faith with the people we love, and Pamana is my way of helping those traditions reach a new generation.
